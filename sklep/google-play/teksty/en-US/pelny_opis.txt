At the edge of an ancient forest lies Zapadlina, a village the world has forgotten. A young settler named Maciek comes down into the valley to build his hut. He does not yet know that the forest remembers everything – and that one day it will come for what it is owed.

Little village is an interactive story inspired by Slavic folklore. You follow three generations of one family: Maciek, his grandson Jaromir and Jaromir’s daughter Marysia. Every choice you make – whom to trust, what to take and what to give back – shapes their fate.

• A prologue and 20 chapters – a whole novel to read
• Different endings, depending on the choices you make
• Black-and-white scenes in a hand-drawn style that come alive as you read
• Creatures from Slavic folklore: the Lord of the Forest, Lady Midday, mamunas, the strzyga and more, recorded in Jaromir’s notes as you meet them
• Book of Tales: start again from any chapter you have reached and see which endings you have found
• Adjustable text size, light and dark reading windows
• Automatic saving after every page
• No ads

The prologue and the first four chapters are free. The rest of the story is unlocked with a single purchase – no subscription.

Available in English, German, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Italian, Czech, Slovak, Japanese and Polish.
